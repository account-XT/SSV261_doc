IR Drop Aware Placement

The IR drop aware placement flow mitigates IR drop hotspots by spreading the simultaneous switching aggressors to different locations. This reduces current requirements at the hot spot regions, and therefore decreasing IR drop.

In this flow, Innovus, Voltus, and Tempus tools work in tandem. Voltus runs the hotspot debugger after performing rail analysis to identify IR hot spots. Tempus runs timing and helps identify simultaneous switching aggressors, and Innovus spreads aggressors in the vicinity without degrading timing.
