unspecify_ilm

unspecify_ilm
[-help]
-cell cellName

Unspecifies the ILM cell.

Note: The ILM is not supported for Signoff in Tempus.

Parameters

-help Prints out the command usage.

-cell cellName

Specifies the name of the block to unspecify.
