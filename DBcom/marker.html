Product Version 26.10, May 2026

marker

Parent Objects

design, root

Definition

A violation marker. All markers have a .bbox and .originator set, and optionally .polygon for polygon shapes. Other attributes depend on the marker. Markers created by internal check or report commands will have .originator != external and have a .layer, .type, .subtype, .message, and .message_id defined by those commands. Markers created with read_markers or create_marker will have .originator == external and will have .layer, .user_type, .user_subtype, .user_originator, and .message based on the external input.
