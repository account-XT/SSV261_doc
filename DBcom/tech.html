Product Version 26.10, May 2026

tech

Parent Objects

via_def, route_rule, base_cell, design, pg_base_pin, root, site, via_def_rule, layer, base_pin

Definition

The LEF or OA technology and library data used by a given design. It has all the layers, base_cells, route_rules, via_defs, etc. for a given process technology node. It may be used by one or more design objects.
