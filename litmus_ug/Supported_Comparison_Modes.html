Supported Comparison Modes

Timing-Aware Constraint Comparison (1D2S)

Timing-aware comparison analyzes constraints in the context of design timing topology, including clock trees and timing paths. It detects:

  • Clock hierarchy mismatches
  • Period, latency, and uncertainty differences
  • Generated clock inconsistencies
  • Exception intent conflicts

Results are reported using rule-based signoff checks.

Timing-Unaware Constraint Comparison (2SDC)

Timing-unaware comparison performs a pure SDC-to-SDC comparison, independent of timing graph analysis. Its key characteristics include:

  • Faster execution
  • Structural and semantic SDC comparison
  • Useful for early validation and auditing

Note: 2SDC Compare provides a timing‑unaware, high‑performance constraint comparison with structured tabular reporting, automatically identifying mismatches, unique constraints, and anchor points for scalable analysis of large designs.
