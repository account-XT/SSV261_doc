Constraints Promotion

Modern SoC development brings together a large number of independently designed IP blocks, each with its own timing requirements, clock structures, and exception constraints captured in SDC format. During integration, these block-level SDCs must be correctly interpreted, migrated, verified, and merged into a unified SoC-level constraint set. Manual promotion of block constraints is time-consuming and error-prone. Missing clocks, incorrect IO delays, inconsistent exceptions, or mismatched case analyses result in SoC timing closure failure. Litmus Constraint Promotion provides an automated, structured, auditable flow that promotes block constraints into the SoC design in a deterministic and rules‑driven manner.

In Litmus, Constraint Promotion is the process of guided migration of block‑level constraints to the SoC-level environment. It ensures:

  • Consistency between block and SoC intent
  • Completeness of SoC-level SDCs
  • Auditability via Constraints Promotion Tags (CP-tags)
  • Deterministic conflict resolution
  • Reduced human effort and mistakes

The following figure highlights how constraint promotion acts as a unifying bridge across both SoC integration and implementation phases.

  • During integration, Litmus ensures that timing intent, defined at the block level, is accurately propagated into the SoC timing environment before handoff to signoff checks.
  • As the design moves into physical implementation, Innovus partitions consume the promoted SoC‑level constraints, and Litmus again validates and promotes hierarchical constraints during partition and top‑level assembly.

By appearing consistently at each key transition: RTL to netlist, block to SoC, and partition to top-level, constraint promotion ensures that timing intent is preserved, conflicts are eliminated early, and the final chip-level SDC reflects a coherent, validated view of the entire hierarchy.
