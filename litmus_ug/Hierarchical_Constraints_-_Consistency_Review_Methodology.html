Hierarchical Constraints - Consistency Review Methodology

The recommended methodology for constraints review consists of the following:

  • Design setup - review libraries and designs.
  • Design Initialization - review libraries lint checks.
  • Constraints linting - review constraints lint checks.
  • Constraints consistency analysis - review hierarchical clock mapping and constraints hierarchical signoff checks based on the constraints categories.
