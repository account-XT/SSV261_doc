Hierarchical Constraints Signoff Use Model

Verification is an iterative process that can be minimized by streamlined methodology. Constraints hierarchical signoff flow consists of the following:

  • Setup: Design Initialization

    • Defines checkpoints in the flow.

    • Specifies constraints that provide full coverage for the design.

    • Validates correctness of the design and constraints setup or coverage.

    • Provides standard CUI setup support, leveraging common design across Cadence implementation/signoff tools.

  • Analysis

    • Captures timing intent with constraints (hierarchical) signoff checks.

    • Provides additional timing intent specifications that configure rules to meet design methodology.

  • Review: Reporting and Diagnosis

    • Provides concise and less noisy reports for your review.

    • Annotates failure modes.

    • Provides access to analyze information via CUI DB access commands.
