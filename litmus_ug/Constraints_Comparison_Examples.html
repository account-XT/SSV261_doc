Example 8 - Physically Exclusive Clock Exceptions

In cases where exception constraints specify physically exclusive launch and capture clock pairs using -from and -to, no timing violation is reported by analysis. 2‑SDC Compare still detects and reports this constraint difference, ensuring such intent mismatches are visible during constraint comparison even when timing behavior is unaffected. 

1D2S performs a timing‑aware comparison, so when the launch and capture clocks specified in -from and -to are physically exclusive by design, no timing violation is reported and the constraint difference is effectively filtered out. As a result, 1D2S does not surface this mismatch, even though the constraint definitions differ, whereas 2‑SDC Compare reports it explicitly.

Reference SDC:

create_clock -name CLK -period 10 [get_ports clk]

create_clock -name CLK2 -period 20 [get_ports clk] -add

Target SDC:

set inst blk/

create_clock -name CLK -period 10 [get_ports clk]

create_clock -name CLK2 -period 20 [get_ports clk] -add

set_max_delay 3 -from [get_clocks CLK] -through [get_pins ${inst}dffout/DATA] -to [get_clocks CLK2]

1D2S Compare:

2SDC Compare:

When exception constraints specify launch and capture clocks that are physically exclusive by design, no timing violation is reported. In this scenario, the constraint CONST_COMP_EXC11 is therefore ignored by timing analysis. However, 2‑SDC Compare still captures and reports a constraint difference, ensuring that mismatches in constraint intent between Reference and Target SDCs are not missed.
