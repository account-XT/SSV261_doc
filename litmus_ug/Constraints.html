Constraints

Constraints provide timing intent specifications that a design must meet through optimization. Synopsys Design Constraints (SDC) format is the standard for writing constraints in the industry, which conforms to Tcl syntax.

The following table lists the constraints categories, and the common commands based on them.

Categories Commands

Design rule constraints

Others

The recommended order for reviewing constraints based on their categories is:

  1. Case analysis
  2. Clock
  3. IO
  4. Exception
  5. Others (including Environmental and Design rule)
