Constraints Signoff

Litmus uses rules to ensure that the constraints are written correctly with the RTL and applied correctly throughout the design, implementation, and signoff stages. The constraints signoff verifies the correctness and completeness of constraints at the top and/or block-level. It also identifies the missing constraints, exception overlaps, and case analysis conflicts. It runs the following checks:

  • Clock checks
  • IO checks
  • Exception checks
  • Case Analysis checks
