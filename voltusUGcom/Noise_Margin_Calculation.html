Noise Margin Calculation

Noise margin calculation allows you to determine the allowable noise voltage on the input of a gate so that the output will not be affected. It is the amount of noise a circuit can resist without affecting its operation. These values are determined to ensure that the false noise signals are not causing any undesired operation. The noise values can be compared with the noise margins and if they are within the margins provided, it indicates the ability of the circuit to operate in the presence of noise.

Here,

Voh (minimum output high voltage) = VDD_driver - VSS_receiver

Vol (maximum output low voltage) = VSS_driver - VSS_receiver

Vih (minimum input high voltage) = (1-noise_limit)*(VDD_receiver - VSS_receiver)

Vil (maximum input low voltage) = noise_limit*(VDD_receiver - VSS_receiver)

The following is the criteria for noise margin calculation:

Margin_High = NMH (Voh - Vih) - signal noise - guardband > 0

Margin_Low = NML (Vil - Vol) - signal noise - guardband > 0
