Design Sanity Checks

Voltus lets you check the design data before performing power and rail analysis so that any missing or inconsistent data can be detected earlier in the design process.

You can perform the following design sanity checks:

  • Check the design
  • Check the timing library data
  • Verify the connectivity of the design
  • Verify the power vias
  • Verify the power and ground shorts
