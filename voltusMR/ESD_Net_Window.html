ESD Net Window

Displays the From and To sections for the net selected in the Rule Detail form.

 To open the ESD Net Window form, select a net in the bottom section of the Rule Detail form.

This form has two parts:

  • The top section displays the details of the From Clamps/Bumps.
  • The bottom section displays the details of the To Clamps/Bumps. You can select a row from the top section to display the corresponding To Clamps/Bumps in the bottom section. When you select a “To Clamp/Bump”, the GUI will highlight the fly lines between the From Clamp/Bump and the To Clamp/Bump.

You can specify a pattern in the Pattern field, and click the From Clamps/Bumps or To Clamps/Bumps button to filter the rule records according to the specified pattern. For example, if you specify ESDCLAMP in the Pattern field, and click To Clamps/Bumps, all the bumps/clamps with ESDCLAMP text in their names will be displayed in the bottom section.

For information on the From and To sections of the net, refer to the "ESD Analysis" chapter in the Voltus User Guide.
