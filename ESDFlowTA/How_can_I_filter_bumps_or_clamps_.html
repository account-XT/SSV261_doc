How can I filter bumps or clamps?

You can filter and view the effective resistance and pass/fail status of the desired “from bump/clamp” or “to clamp/bump”.  To search for a bump/clamp among a large number of bump-clamp pairs:

  1. In the Net form, enter the name of the bump or clamp to filter in the Pattern field.
  2. Click From Clamps/Bumps to filter the top section or To Clamps/Bumps to filter the bottom section.
