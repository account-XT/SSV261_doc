How to view effective resistance check reports for different rule types?

To view the effective resistance check reports for different rule types:

  1. Select Power Rail - ESD Results.
    The ESD Summary form appears.
  2. Click the browse button to access the ESD directory under the state directory to load results, and click Load.
  3. Click on a specific rule to view a summary of that rule.
    The Rule Detail form appears.

  4. Select a net (resistance check)/domain (EM) in the lower section of the form.
    The Net (resistance check)/domain (EM) form appears.

  5. Select a row to highlight the flight lines between the “from clamps/bumps” and the “to clamps/bumps” in the layout window. Green flight lines indicate passing paths and red flight lines indicate failing paths.
