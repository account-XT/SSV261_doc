CONST_CLK_DEF46

NAME

CONST_CLK_DEF46 (CONSTRAINT_SIGNOFF)

MESSAGE

Missing generated clock on the clock generator circuit

DEFAULT SEVERITY

Warning

DESCRIPTION

This rule flags a violation for each case where Q pin of any flop is connected to the clock pin of another flop or to an output port (without set_output_delay), through any combinational logic and create_generated_clock statement is not defined on the output pin of the generator flop.

Reporting (message and attribute): Missing generated clock on the output pin (Q pin) of the flop driving a clock pin (clock_pin) /output port (output_port)

This rule has specific rule occurrence attributes for the details about the violation.

Attribute

Type

Description

occurrence_id unsigned Occurrence ID
scope string Design Name
output_pin object Clock gating enable pin
clock_pin_or_output_ports object_list clock_pin_or_output_ports

EXAMPLES

Scenario 1: The Q pin of the flop drives a clock pin

Criteria for Q pin selection:

  • Consider all Q pins that drive clock pin
  • Ignore Q pins that do not drive any clock pin

Sample report:

CONST_CLK_DEF46 : Missing generated clock on the clock generator circuit
Severity: Warning                        Occurrence: 1
Fail:  Missing generated clock on the output pin (ff_1/Q ) of the flop driving a clock pin (ff_2/clk2)

Reporting Scenarios:

  • Case 1: No clock at the fanin of ff_1, but CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta1)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q]

  • Case 2: CC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, no CC/CGC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta2)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_d -period 5 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 3: CGC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, no CC/CGC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta2)
    • SDC: create_generated_clock -name gclk1 -source [get_pins ff_1/CK] -divide_by 1 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 4: CC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta4)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q] create_clock -name clk2 -period 5 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 5: CGC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta5)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q]
      create_generated_clock -name gclk1 -source [get_pins ff_1/CK] -divide_by 1 [get_pins ff_1/D]


Scenario 2: The Q pin of the flop drives an output port


Criteria for output port selection:

  • Consider all output ports that do not have Set Output Direct (SOD) defined on it
  • Ignore output ports that have SOD defined.

Sample report:

CONST_CLK_DEF46 : Missing generated clock on the clock generator circuit
Severity: Warning                        Occurrence: 1
Fail:  Missing generated clock on the output pin (ff_1/Q ) of the flop driving a output port (Q1) 

Reporting Scenarios:

  • Case 6: No clock at the fanin of ff_1, but CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta6)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q]

  • Case 7: CC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, no CC/CGC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta7)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_d -period 5 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 8: CGC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, no CC/CGC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta8)
    • SDC: create_generated_clock -name gclk1 -source [get_pins ff_1/CK] -divide_by 1 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 9: CC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta9)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q] create_clock -name clk2 -period 5 [get_pins ff_1/D]

  • Case 10: CGC at the fanin (D) of ff_1, CC at the Q pin of ff_1 (dofile.cta10)
    • SDC: create_clock -name clk_ff1_q -period 10 [get_pins ff_1/Q] create_generated_clock -name gclk1 -source [get_pins ff_1/CK] -divide_by 1 [get_pins ff_1/D]
