My Products

The My Products section lists all products installed at your site or computer. Use it to select or deselect a product from this list, which will change the order in which content appears in the welcome page, search results, and results from the Cadence Application Support & Knowledge (ASK) portal.

You can also change products from  icon that is displayed when you are on the Doc Assistant welcome page.

When there are multiple documentation releases added in the Releases pane, you can set unique product preferences for each release:

  1. Select the required release from the drop-down list.
  2. Select the required products for the release.
  3. Click Save to apply your preferences.

 


Your product preferences are saved. These settings are applied when running search on documents from the current release or across multiple releases.
