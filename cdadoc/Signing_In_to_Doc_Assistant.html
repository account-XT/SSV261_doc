Signing In to Doc Assistant

In Online mode, you can sign in to Doc Assistant using your email ID. This helps you personalize Doc Assistant settings.

To sign in to Doc Assistant:

  1. Click the Sign In () icon in the menu bar. The Sign In dialog box appears.
  2. Type your email ID.
  3. Click Get OTP.
    An OTP is sent to your email ID.
  4. Type the OTP within 60 seconds of receiving it. If there is any delay, click Skip to return to the home page and wait for 60 seconds before signing in again.
  5. Select I have read and agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  6. Click Log In.

You are now signed into Doc Assistant.
