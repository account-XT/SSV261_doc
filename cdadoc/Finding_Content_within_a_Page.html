Finding Content within a Page

The Find feature allows you to quickly locate specific words or sentences within any page in Doc Assistant.

To search within a page:

  1. Press Ctrl + F or select Find In Page from the hamburger menu to display the Find box. 

  2. Type the search term in the Find box and press Enter. All instances of the searched keyword in the page are highlighted.

  3. Type your word or phrase in the Find box.

  4. Press Enter to start the search.

  5. Review your results:

    • All matching instances will be highlighted on the page.

    • Use the and arrows to jump between matches. 

If no matches are found, the Find box turns red.
