Sending Feedback

You can share your input or report an issue using the built-in feedback form. Follow the steps below to submit your feedback: 

  1. Open the Provide your feedback! form using one of the following methods:
    • Use the button in the topic viewer to share your feedback on the topic content.
    • Click the  button at the bottom of the TOC to share feedback about the product.
    • Select Application Feedback from the hamburger menu to share feedback about Doc Assistant.
  2. Select the feedback type. Select one of the following options based on your experience:
    • Helped in learning – The content improved your understanding.
    • Resolved my query – The information answered your question.
    • Needs review – The content needs an update because it was inaccurate, outdated, or unclear.
  3. Type your comments in the Your Comment field. Type specific feedback, suggestions, or issues related to the content or search result.
  4. Type your corporate email ID. This is a mandatory field to help us follow up if required.
  5. Click Submit Feedback to send your input.

Your feedback is routed to the documentation or support team for further review and action.

When feedback is submitted offline, it is securely stored locally and automatically syncs when you reconnect to the internet.
