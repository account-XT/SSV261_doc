Focus Area

The Focus Area page displays details about the most visited product feature topics based on your browsing history. Links to these topics along with the product name and topic type are displayed in a table below the chart.

The page includes:

  • A visual chart displaying your top feature interests
  • A detailed table below the chart containing:
    • Direct links to each topic
    • Associated product names
    • Topic categories

This personalized view helps you quickly navigate to the features and information most relevant to your work.
